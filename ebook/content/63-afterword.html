Afterword

Every period of cheap, powerful new technology produces the same two reactions. Some people wait to see what the experts will do with it. Others notice that the experts don’t know their corner of the world nearly as well as they do, and get to work.

The eight models in this book are different routes to the same destination: a business built on something AI can’t generate for itself. That might be your judgment as a professional, your method as a coach, your taste as a fan, your understanding of a group’s rituals, data you gather with care and consent, or the trust of a community you build in public. The technology is the same for everyone. What you bring to it isn’t.

You don’t need to master all eight. You need one model, one niche, one offer, and ten honest conversations. Then you improve a little every week. Start with what you know, sell to people who already pay, and keep an eye on your model’s trap. The rest of this book will be waiting when you’re ready to stack the next one.

The Workbook that follows is where reading turns into doing. Fill it in with a pencil, change your mind, and fill it in again.

Same tools as everyone else. Different knowledge. That’s the whole advantage, and it’s yours.
